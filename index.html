Card combat

Tip the scale. Win the fight.

Hit an open lane to tip the scale your way. Enemy hits push it back. Reach a ten point lead to win.

Extra damage earns golden teeth to help in later fights.

Blood
Sacrifice cards on your board to play stronger ones.
Bones
Earn one per card death. Save them to play bone-cost cards.
Candles
Your lives. Lose a fight, lose a candle. Run out and start again.

How to play

Draw. Play. Attack.

Choose your cards and lanes. Ring the bell to attack automatically.

  1. 01Start of turn

    Draw a card.

    Start each turn with a card from your deck or the fodder pile. Fodder gives you a free card to sacrifice.
